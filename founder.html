Founder

Shailendra Saurav

Building practical technology for founders and small teams. Focused on clarity, automation that actually works, and tools that reduce operational friction across sales, marketing and trading.

Revenue360s grew out of the same principle as Stock360s: give people who do the work themselves something clear and usable instead of another complex stack they have to assemble.

The work has included full-stack systems for lead sources, outreach sequences, ad integrations (including Meta CAPI), and a single CRM view, plus related products in stock analytics and other areas under the 360s Group.

Based in Dhanbad, Jharkhand. Preference for simple, low-overhead tools that a small team can actually run without dedicated operations staff.

Other products: Stock360s · Carbon360s
