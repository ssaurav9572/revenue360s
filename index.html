FAQs

Who is Revenue360s for?

Solo entrepreneurs and small teams (1–15 people) who handle sales and marketing themselves and want one platform instead of five tools.

What services are included?

Lead discovery and ICP scoring, personalized email + voice note sequences, Meta / Google / X / YouTube ads with CAPI and conversion tracking, WhatsApp qualification, and a single CRM. You can buy the entire package or configure only the parts you need (for example outreach only, or ads + CRM).

How long does full setup take?

With full setup (ICP, sources, sequences, ad accounts, CRM, and tracking), delivery is typically 24–48 hours after we have the required access and details.

How is it unique? Is it just another AI or LLM tool?

No. The system is not a generic LLM wrapper. ICP definition, targeting methods, sequence structure, and detailed targeting rules are drawn from historical datasets that have produced strong cold-email open and reply rates. The AI configures and personalizes inside those proven patterns rather than inventing them from scratch.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

Yes. Meta ads send leads directly to WhatsApp. The system qualifies them and pushes only relevant ones into your CRM with Meta CAPI.

Can I configure workflows with AI?

Yes. Each step (ICP, sources, email sequences, voice notes, ad rules) can be adjusted by chatting with the AI. No manual setup required.

Is there a single CRM?

Yes. Leads from outreach, Meta, X, Google and YouTube all land in one CRM. Export to CSV or Excel anytime.

What tracking is included for email and ads?

Email: opens, replies, and status per lead. Ads: spend, leads, CPL, CPM, CTR, landing-page analytics, ROAS and ROI where the platform supports them (Meta, Google, YouTube, X).
