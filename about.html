About Revenue360s

Revenue360s is a sales and marketing platform built for solo founders and small teams who handle everything themselves.

Instead of stitching together Apollo, LinkedIn, a separate email tool, Meta Ads Manager, a WhatsApp CRM and a spreadsheet, the platform keeps the full motion in one place: find scored leads, send unique emails and voice notes, run ads that feed into the same CRM, and track replies and meetings.

What it focuses on

Lead discovery and ICP scoring, personalized email + voice note sequences, Meta / Google / X / YouTube ads with CAPI and conversion tracking, WhatsApp qualification, and a single CRM. You can take the entire package or configure only the parts you need.

ICP definition, targeting methods, sequence structure and detailed targeting rules are drawn from historical datasets that have produced strong cold-email open and reply rates. The system configures and personalizes inside those proven patterns rather than relying on a generic LLM to invent them.

Who it is for

Founders and small teams (typically 1–15 people) in India who currently do sales and marketing themselves and want fewer tools and less manual follow-up.

What it is not

It is not a replacement for a full enterprise CRM or a large marketing team. It is a practical system for people who need the core motion — find, message, advertise, book — without hiring more people.

It is part of the 360s Group alongside Stock360s and Carbon360s.
